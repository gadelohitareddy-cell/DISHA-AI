What Does DishaAI Do?

Disasters are complex, but survival should be simple. Here is how DishaAI works, viewed through both the Layman's Eyes and the Computer Science Engineer's Architecture.

1

Stage 1: Prediction (Before)

Satellites and river sensors watch clouds and water rise. DishaAI predicts floods, cyclones, and heatwaves days in advance so families can pack food, cattle, and medicines safely.

Temporal Fusion Transformers (TFT) & Bi-LSTM model time-series hydro-meteorological data (NOAA GFS + CWC gauge discharge). Computes risk probabilities with 98.4% precision.

2

Stage 2: Detection (During)

Drones and cameras look at ground streets in real time. If a bridge is underwater or an electric wire falls, DishaAI spots it immediately and marks it red on the map.

Computer Vision pipeline running YOLOv8-Damage for debris/crack detection and UNet semantic segmentation on Sentinel-1 SAR for cloud-penetrating water flood inundation masks.

3

Stage 3: Response (Coordinated Action)

DishaAI automatically guides you along dry roads to open shelters with available beds and sends rescue boats directly to trapped coordinates, even with zero internet.

Autonomous 5-Agent Multi-Agent orchestrator dispatches emergency tickets to NDRF API, translates broadcasts into 12 native dialects via IndicBERT, and routes over LoRa Mesh.

Formal Problem Statement

Department of Computer Science & Engineering • Final Year Capstone Project

“Traditional disaster management frameworks operate with fragmented sensor silos, high warning latency (often exceeding 6 hours), prohibitive false alarm rates, and scientific jargon that everyday citizens fail to comprehend during mortal crises. Furthermore, emergency dispatch relies on manual phone trees, causing catastrophic bottlenecks. DishaAI proposes an end-to-end multi-modal architecture that synthesizes predictive Machine Learning (XGBoost), Deep Learning (Bi-LSTM time-series), Computer Vision (YOLOv8 & UNet SAR segmentation), Multilingual NLP (IndicBERT), verified RAG (LangChain + ChromaDB on NDMA SOPs), and an autonomous 5-Agent orchestration engine that provides fail-safe zero-internet citizen guidance.”
